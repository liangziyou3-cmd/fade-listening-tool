QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Good to know before you press play.

Here is what Fade prepares, what you can change, and what to expect from a lesson.

How does Fade turn a video into a lesson?

Fade Agent uses available subtitles or a transcript to build timed subtitle lines and translations, identify useful language, add culture notes and comprehension checks, then validate the lesson content.

Does Fade transcribe every video?

Not yet. The Agent relies on subtitles or a transcript that it can access; it does not download the video or transcribe its audio. The video must also be playable in the embedded player.

Which languages can I learn?

Spanish and French are currently supported as video languages. Each has its own language-specific analysis standards. You can choose from the available translation languages.

What is the difference between Teacher and Student mode?

Teacher mode lets you review and edit the Agent's lesson content before sharing it with students. Student mode skips that review and goes straight to video learning.

What can teachers review or edit?

Teachers can edit sentence translations, add or remove language-element annotations, manage culture notes, and change quiz questions and answers.

How does the student lesson work?

The video and subtitles stay in sync. Students can show the translation, adjust the subtitle-element hide or highlight strategy, explore culture notes, complete checkpoints, and review answers in their report.
