QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

A focused lesson, with room to listen.

Fade is designed for one clear practice session at a time.

Who is Fade for?

Fade is for intermediate learners who can follow some real speech and want more listening practice outside class.

What do I need to begin?

Paste a YouTube video link. Fade Agent uses the link to prepare the listening lesson.

Will the subtitles disappear completely?

No. Fade hides selected phrases based on the lesson level. You can reveal a phrase when you need it.

How is the lesson content prepared?

Fade Agent analyzes the YouTube video and prepares synced subtitles, translation, difficult-word support, and a short check before practice begins.

What happens after the video?

A short checkpoint quiz checks the main idea and leaves you with useful phrases to review.

Which languages are available?

The current demo includes Spanish, English, and French. Fade Agent can prepare listening lessons for the languages supported in its analysis workflow.
